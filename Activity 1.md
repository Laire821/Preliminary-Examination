> 💌 *“A good presentation does not only catch attention—it guides the audience toward the message.”*

For this activity, I created a promotional presentation for a **Free Coffee** event at Arrupe Hall, Booth 7. I applied different presentation design principles to make the information visually appealing, easy to understand, and effective in communicating the event details.

<br>

**୨୧ ─────────────── ୨୧**

# 🎀 𝓓𝓮𝓼𝓲𝓰𝓷 𝓟𝓻𝓲𝓷𝓬𝓲𝓹𝓵𝓮𝓼 𝓐𝓹𝓹𝓵𝓲𝓮𝓭 🎀

<br>

<tr>
<td align="center" width="50%">

## 💙 **01 — CONTRAST**

The combination of **deep blue, cream, and warm brown/gold** creates strong visual contrast.

The large blue **“FREE COFFEE”** text immediately stands out against the light background, making the main message easy to notice.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Contrast helped important information stand out and prevented the poster from looking flat or difficult to read.

</td>

<td align="center" width="50%">

## 🎀 **02 — VISUAL HIERARCHY**

Different font sizes and weights were used to establish an order of importance.

The audience notices **“FREE COFFEE”** first, followed by the location, date, coffee images, and supporting details.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Hierarchy allowed viewers to understand the most important information quickly without having to read everything.

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td align="center" width="50%">

## 🌷 **03 — BALANCE**

The two coffee cups placed near the center create a balanced composition.

The large blue curved shape at the bottom also helps visually support the coffee cups and prevents the lower part of the poster from feeling empty.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Balance made the poster feel stable, organized, and visually pleasing.

</td>

<td align="center" width="50%">

## 🦢 **04 — ALIGNMENT**

The major elements are arranged around a strong **central alignment**.

The logo, location, headline, date, coffee images, and supporting message follow a clear visual structure.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Alignment created order and made the information easier for the audience to follow.

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td align="center" width="50%">

## 💌 **05 — PROXIMITY**

Related information was grouped together.

The **location** appears near the top, while the **date** is placed directly below the main headline. The coffee images are grouped with the “first come, first served” information.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Proximity helped viewers understand which pieces of information belong together.

</td>

<td align="center" width="50%">

## ✨ **06 — REPETITION & CONSISTENCY**

The design consistently uses the same visual language through the **blue and warm neutral color palette**, italicized text, rounded shapes, and repeated visual accents.

### ✦ How it improved the design:

Consistency made the poster look cohesive and intentional instead of having unrelated design elements.

</td>
</tr>

# 🪞 𝓣𝓱𝓮 𝓡𝓸𝓵𝓮 𝓸𝓯 𝓦𝓱𝓲𝓽𝓮 𝓢𝓹𝓪𝓬𝓮

<br>

The light cream background provides sufficient **white space** around the main elements.

Instead of filling every area with text or graphics, empty space was intentionally maintained around the headline, date, and coffee images.

### 🌸 This helped by:

✨ Improving readability  
✨ Preventing visual clutter  
✨ Making the main elements more noticeable  
✨ Giving the design a clean and modern appearance

<br>

**୨୧ ─────────────── ୨୧**

# ☕ 𝓥𝓲𝓼𝓾𝓪𝓵 𝓔𝓵𝓮𝓶𝓮𝓷𝓽𝓼 𝓪𝓷𝓭 𝓘𝓶𝓪𝓰𝓮𝓻𝔂

<br>

The coffee cup images serve as the main **visual focal point** of the lower section.

Using realistic images of the drinks makes the advertisement more engaging and allows viewers to immediately understand what the promotion is about.

The curved blue shape behind the cups also creates **depth and visual separation**, allowing the coffee images to stand out from the background.

<br>

# 💙 **COLOR PALETTE**

<br>

| 🎨 Color | Purpose |
|:---:|:---|
| 🔵 **Blue** | Creates emphasis and represents the main visual identity |
| 🤍 **Cream** | Provides a clean background and improves readability |
| 🟤 **Brown / Gold** | Connects visually with the coffee theme |
| 🧡 **Warm Gold** | Highlights supporting text and decorative elements |

<br>

The color palette was intentionally limited so that the poster would remain **cohesive and easy to recognize**.

<br>

**୨୧ ─────────────── ୨୧**

# 🌹 𝓗𝓸𝔀 𝓣𝓱𝓮 𝓟𝓻𝓲𝓷𝓬𝓲𝓹𝓵𝓮𝓼 𝓘𝓶𝓹𝓻𝓸𝓿𝓮𝓭 𝓜𝔂 𝓟𝓻𝓮𝓼𝓮𝓷𝓽𝓪𝓽𝓲𝓸𝓷

<br>

The design principles worked together to transform simple event information into a visually engaging promotional poster.

### 🎀 **Contrast**
made the important information immediately visible.

### 🌷 **Hierarchy**
guided the audience from the main message to the supporting details.

### 🦢 **Balance**
created a stable and pleasing composition.

### 💌 **Alignment**
kept the information organized and structured.

### ✨ **Proximity**
made relationships between information easier to understand.

### ☕ **Imagery**
made the promotion more engaging and connected the design to the coffee theme.

### 🪞 **White Space**
prevented the poster from becoming overcrowded.

<br>

# 💋 **THE RESULT**

### *Clear information.*
### *Strong visual impact.*
### *Easy-to-follow composition.*

<br>

The final design communicates the **Free Coffee** promotion quickly and effectively. The audience can immediately identify **what is being offered, where it is happening, when it is happening, and the important “first come, first served” condition.**
